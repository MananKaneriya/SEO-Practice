AI App 1 for SG Highway Offices & Showrooms

AI App 1 answers questions on WhatsApp, website chat and Instagram 24/7, in English, Hindi and Gujarati, using your own service lists, price lists, FAQs and brochures. It collects the basics of each enquiry, such as what the customer is looking for and how to reach them, so that your salesperson's first call starts with context instead of a blank page. AI App 2 – Lead Scoring & Follow-up for Sales Teams

Many SG Highway teams handle online leads and personal visits side by side. AI App 2 collects the online ones from website forms, WhatsApp, IndiaMART, Facebook and Google Ads into a single pipeline and scores each lead hot, warm or cold, so attention goes to the people most likely to buy. Automatic reminders and WhatsApp and email templates keep follow-ups on schedule, it can draft a quotation or proposal from a short note, and managers see a daily sales report.
