Our WhatsApp AI chatbot (AI App 1) answers customers on WhatsApp, website chat and Instagram around the clock, in English, Hindi and Gujarati. AI App 2 – Lead Management & Sales Follow-up in Ahmedabad

Enquiries in Ahmedabad rarely arrive in one place. They come from website forms, WhatsApp, IndiaMART, Facebook and Google Ads, often on the same afternoon. Our lead management software (AI App 2) collects all of them in a single list, scores every lead as hot, warm or cold, and sets automatic reminders so that nobody is forgotten after the first call.

Your team gets ready-made WhatsApp and email templates for the most common follow-ups, and AI App 2 can draft a quotation or proposal from a short note about what the customer asked for. Owners and managers receive a daily sales report, so they can see which leads are moving and which are stuck without chasing each salesperson for an update.
