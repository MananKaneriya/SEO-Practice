How Setup Works Across Gujarat

Setup is the same in a small town as in a big city, because everything happens online.

  1. Book a free demo. We learn about your business and show the app working on questions or leads like yours.
  2. Share your information. For AI App 1 that means your product list, price list, FAQs and brochures. For AI App 2 it means the channels your leads come from.
  3. We set it up online. We connect WhatsApp, website chat, Instagram or your lead sources and test everything with you.
  4. Training and support. We train your staff over video call and keep supporting you afterwards. On-site onboarding is available in major cities such as Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara and Rajkot.
