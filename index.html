Ortheon AI – AI Apps for Customer Support and Sales

Ortheon AI builds AI software that helps Gujarat businesses sell more and support customers faster. Our two apps work on the channels your customers already use – WhatsApp, your website and Instagram – so you can answer enquiries quickly, follow up on time and stop losing business to slow replies.

AI App 1 – AI Customer Support Assistant

AI App 1 is an AI chatbot that answers customer questions 24/7 on WhatsApp, website chat and Instagram. It replies instantly in English, Hindi and Gujarati, learns from your product list, price list, FAQs and brochures, books appointments and site visits, and hands the chat to a member of your staff when a person is needed. The result is fewer missed enquiries, faster replies and less load on your team.

See how AI App 1 works

AI App 2 – AI Sales and Lead Management App

AI App 2 captures leads from website forms, WhatsApp, IndiaMART, Facebook and Google Ads in one place, scores each lead as hot, warm or cold, and reminds your sales team who to follow up with and when. It also writes quotation and proposal drafts from a short note and sends owners and managers a daily sales report. The result is more leads converted, no forgotten follow-ups and a clear sales pipeline.
