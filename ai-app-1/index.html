AI App 1 – AI Customer Support Assistant

AI App 1 is an AI chatbot from Ortheon AI that answers customer questions 24/7 on WhatsApp, website chat and Instagram. It means fewer missed enquiries, faster replies and less load on your staff.

What Is AI App 1?

Customers expect an answer within minutes, even at night or on a holiday. AI App 1 is a customer support assistant that replies instantly on the channels your customers already use. You teach it about your business from your own product list, price list, FAQs and brochures, so its answers match what you actually sell. When a conversation needs a person, it passes the chat to a member of your staff instead of guessing.

Key Features

  • Replies instantly in English, Hindi and Gujarati
  • Works on WhatsApp, website chat and Instagram
  • Learns from your product list, price list, FAQs and brochures
  • Books appointments and site visits, and sends reminders
  • Hands the chat to a human staff member when needed
  • Dashboard showing the most-asked questions and missed leads

Who AI App 1 Is For

AI App 1 suits businesses that get the same kinds of questions from customers again and again.

  • Retail shops and showrooms: answer questions about products, prices and availability without tying up the counter.
  • Clinics and coaching classes: answer common questions, book appointments and send reminders.
  • Real estate developers: share project details and book site visits.
  • Manufacturers and service businesses: handle repeat product and service questions and pass serious enquiries to your team.

FAQs – AI App 1

Which channels does AI App 1 work on?
AI App 1 answers customers on WhatsApp, on website chat and on Instagram, so you do not have to check three inboxes to keep up with enquiries.
Can AI App 1 reply in Gujarati and Hindi?
Yes. It replies instantly in English, Hindi and Gujarati, so customers can write in the language they are most comfortable with.
How does AI App 1 learn about my business?
It learns from the material you already have: your product list, price list, FAQs and brochures. We help you load them during setup, and you can update them whenever your offer changes.
What happens when a customer needs a real person?
AI App 1 can hand the chat over to a member of your staff. Your team picks up the conversation, and the dashboard shows the most-asked questions and any missed leads.
