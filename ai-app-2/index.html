AI App 2 – AI Sales & Lead Management App

AI App 2 is an AI sales tool from Ortheon AI that captures leads from every channel, scores them and reminds your sales team who to follow up with and when. It means more leads converted, no forgotten follow-ups and a clear sales pipeline.

Book a free demo

What Is AI App 2?

Most sales teams do not lose leads because customers are not interested. They lose them because an enquiry arrived on WhatsApp, another came from a Facebook form, and nobody remembered to call back. AI App 2 puts every lead in one place, scores it hot, warm or cold, and tells your team what to do next. Owners and managers get a daily report instead of having to ask each salesperson for an update.

Key Features

  • Collects leads from website forms, WhatsApp, IndiaMART, Facebook and Google Ads in one place
  • AI lead scoring (hot, warm or cold)
  • Automatic follow-up reminders and WhatsApp and email templates
  • Writes quotation and proposal drafts from a short note
  • Daily sales report for owners and managers

Who AI App 2 Is For

AI App 2 is for sales teams that handle many enquiries at once and need a simple way to keep track of them.

  • Real estate teams: keep every buyer enquiry and site-visit follow-up in one list.
  • Manufacturing and B2B trading: track long quotation cycles so that no buyer is forgotten.
  • Education: follow up on admission enquiries on time.
  • Automobile dealers and agencies: see which leads are hot today and who should call them.

FAQs – AI App 2

Which channels can AI App 2 collect leads from?
AI App 2 collects leads from website forms, WhatsApp, IndiaMART, Facebook and Google Ads and puts them in one place, so your team no longer has to copy enquiries between apps and spreadsheets.
What do hot, warm and cold leads mean?
AI App 2 scores every lead as hot, warm or cold so that your team can start with the people most likely to buy and spend less time on enquiries that are not ready yet.
Can AI App 2 help me write quotations?
Yes. You write a short note about what the customer needs, and AI App 2 writes a quotation or proposal draft that you can check, edit and send.
Who receives the daily sales report?
Owners and managers receive a daily sales report, so they can see what happened in the pipeline and which follow-ups are still pending without chasing the team.
