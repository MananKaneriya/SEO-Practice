How to Reply to WhatsApp Enquiries Faster: A Guide for Gujarat Businesses

By Ortheon AI · Published 24 November 2026 · 6 min read

Most customers who message a business on WhatsApp expect a reply within minutes, not hours. If you run a shop, clinic, showroom or sales office in Gujarat, a few simple habits and one or two tools can bring your reply time down without hiring more people. This guide gives you seven practical fixes, a reply-time target to start with, and a plan for this week.

  2. Seven ways to reply faster

Why Reply Speed Matters on WhatsApp

WhatsApp feels like a conversation, not an email. A customer who asks about a product, a clinic appointment or a flat at nine in the evening is often asking two or three other businesses the same question at the same time. The business that replies first with a useful answer usually gets the next message. The business that replies the next morning often finds that the customer has already moved on.

Slow replies also cost you quietly. Nobody sends a complaint when an enquiry goes unanswered. The customer simply stops writing, and you never see the sale you lost. That is why it is worth measuring your reply speed before you try to improve it. Pick a normal weekday, open your chats and note how long the first reply took for each new enquiry. The slowest ones usually arrived in the evening, at the weekend or while your team was busy with customers in the shop.

Seven Ways to Reply Faster

1. Set business hours and an away message

The free WhatsApp Business app lets you set your opening hours, a greeting for new chats and an away message for after hours. These do not answer the question, but they tell the customer that you have seen the message and when to expect a proper reply. Set them up first, because it takes about ten minutes.

2. Save quick replies for your top ten questions

Write down the ten questions your team answers most often, such as timings, location, availability, how to book and what to bring. Save each answer as a quick reply so that staff can send it by typing a short shortcut instead of typing the whole answer again.

3. Use labels to see who is waiting

Labels such as new enquiry, waiting for us and waiting for customer show at a glance which chats need action. During business hours, review the waiting-for-us label every hour so that nothing sits unanswered.

4. Prepare answers in Gujarati, Hindi and English

Customers write in the language they are most comfortable with. If your saved answers exist in all three languages, a staff member does not have to translate under pressure, and the customer gets a reply that feels natural.

5. Give every chat an owner

When three people can see a chat, each of them assumes that another will answer it. Decide who answers new enquiries each hour or each day, and who covers when that person is busy. A simple rota on a whiteboard is enough.

6. Keep your answers in one place

Keep your product list, price list, FAQs and brochures in one shared folder that everyone uses. When something changes, change it once. A quick answer that is out of date causes more trouble than a slow one.

7. Let an AI chatbot take the repeat questions

Even with saved replies, a person still has to notice the message and send the answer. An AI customer support chatbot answers the repeat questions straight away, at any hour, using your own product list and FAQs. It hands the chat to your staff when a person is needed. Your team then spends its time on the conversations that need judgement, such as a custom quotation or an upset customer.

What a Chatbot Should and Should Not Do

A chatbot is good at questions that have a clear, repeatable answer: opening hours, directions, availability, how to book and which documents to bring. It should not make promises on your behalf, such as agreeing a special rate or giving a medical opinion.

Set clear rules for when a chat must go to a person: an upset customer, a request outside your normal offer, or a question the chatbot is not sure about. A good setup also makes it easy for the customer to ask for a person at any point.

FAQs – Replying to WhatsApp Enquiries

How fast should I reply to a WhatsApp enquiry?
As fast as you reasonably can. A good first target during business hours is within five minutes. After hours, send an instant acknowledgement and a full answer early the next morning. Measure your current reply time first, then improve it step by step.
Do I need the WhatsApp Business app to use these tips?
Quick replies, labels, business hours and away messages are features of the WhatsApp Business app, so it is the easiest place to start. The habits in this guide, such as giving every chat an owner, work with any setup.
Will an AI chatbot replace my staff?
No. A chatbot answers the repeat questions and passes the rest to your team. Your staff spend less time typing the same answers and more time on the conversations that need a person.
